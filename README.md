# Q_Blossom
adds the titular *"Blossom"* Katana  
a **VANILLA-COMPATIBLE SCRIPTED WEAPON** that should not have any incompatibilities  
just dont give the item to someone who doesn't have the mod

obtainable via treasured trophies for **1 violium broadsword** and **5 upgrade modules**
or hand crafting while **/admin** is active

### Things to note:
- holding shift during the **[PRIMARY]** combo prevents it from doing any lunge movements
- gaining Charges requires hitting enemies with the **[PRIMARY]**
- using the **[ALT]** skips to near the end of the **[PRIMARY]** combo  
  ** the attacks right after the skip don't grant any Charges
- the Charge indicator is under the cursor, its the bar with the three slots
